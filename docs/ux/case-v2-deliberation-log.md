## Q1 — Should the hero also carry the debt/rotativo evidence?

**Owner:** Editorial Storyteller  
**Challenger:** Editorial Art Director  
**Arbiter:** Creative Director  
**Resolution:** **NO.**

The headline already opens the contradiction. Adding a second evidence module makes the first fold explain the whole case and recreates the previous stacking problem. The rotativo relation belongs in Discovery.

**Status:** resolved.

## Q2 — Should Fernando appear again as a large portrait in the next section?

**Owner:** Experience Designer  
**Challenger:** Editorial Art Director  
**Arbiter:** Creative Director  
**Resolution:** **NO large repeated portrait.**

Hero owns the protagonist image. The later Fernando moment should deepen consequence with goal/context and a smaller product/goal artifact. Repetition would weaken pacing.

## Q3 — Is a full device frame useful for UX comparisons?

**Owner:** Experience Designer  
**Challenger:** Editorial Art Director  
**Arbiter:** Creative Director  
**Resolution:** **NO.**

The decision being compared is the evidence. Device chrome consumes area and made prior versions read like documentation. Use large UI surfaces with attached Before/After labels and an annotation rail.

**Status:** resolved.

---

## Q4 — May architecture be redrawn editorially?

**Owner:** Repository Analyst  
**Challenger:** Editorial Art Director  
**Arbiter:** Readiness Evaluator  
**Resolution:** **YES, if faithful and explicitly labelled.**

The source-of-truth Mermaid/documented components remain authoritative. An HTML/CSS editorial rendering may simplify geometry, but may not add capabilities or imply runtime validation.

**Status:** resolved.

---

## Q5 — Should mobile embed the full prototype?

**Owner:** Experience Designer  
**Challenger:** Accessibility Auditor  
**Arbiter:** Creative Director  
**Resolution:** **NO requirement for iframe on narrow mobile.**

A cramped embedded app reduces usability and can create focus/scroll problems. Mobile should provide a strong launch action plus scope note; desktop/tablet may retain the iframe.

**Status:** resolved.

---

**Arbiter:** Creative Director  
**Resolution:** **Only as progressive enhancement.**

**Status:** resolved.
