# Bússola V3 — binding PRD

**Status:** binding for V3 production  
**Source:** V2 negative evidence + independent post-publication reviews + repository evidence  
**Orchestration:** Agentic Factory Creative Convergence / Deliberative Quality Loop  
**Human oversight:** exception only; final publication gate

## 1. Outcome

Create a public case that feels authored, proportionally controlled and evidence-led at first glance.

A reader should understand three things without fighting the layout:

1. a positive balance did not explain the full financial pressure;
2. the team turned that tension into a hackathon PoC;
3. later UX/content work made key decisions clearer without rewriting the history of the event.

The page must demonstrate design/content craft without visually behaving like a portfolio template, fintech landing page or oversized editorial poster.

## 2. Audience

Primary:
- senior product/design/content/AI practitioners;
- hiring/curation audiences evaluating Kell's craft and systems thinking;
- technical/product peers who need evidence, not marketing claims.

Secondary:
- hackathon participants and people interested in agentic finance/product work.

## 3. One-line retell

> A synthetic-data signal exposed a gap between visible balance and financial pressure; the team turned it into a goal-first agent PoC, then revisited the experience to make choices and consent clearer.

## 4. Narrative jobs

The final case keeps these jobs, but **does not require eight visually equal sections**.

### Opening
Create the contradiction.

**Headline:** “O saldo está no azul. E a dívida?”

### Discovery
Show why the problem changed when the team looked at the data.

### Human consequence
Use Fernando once as the narrative lens, then stop reintroducing him.

### Product thesis
Make the decision sequence clear:
`objetivo → contexto → cenário → decisão → consentimento`.

### What was built
Show technical ambition through a visual architecture artifact, not a 16-cell documentation grid.

### What changed later
Show four UX/content decisions with product evidence large enough to read.

### Prototype
Let the reader inspect the PoC with limits explicit.

### Collective authorship + colophon
Credit the team and close with observed / explored / refined / still hypothesis.

## 5. V3 visual thesis

### **Evidence first. Scale with restraint.**

V3 keeps the useful idea of “what appears / what weighs underneath”, but removes the oversized-poster execution.

The visual system should feel like a **designed case file**:
- human;
- evidence;
- product states;
- technical artifact;
- concise annotations.

Not:
- giant type as the main source of personality;
- huge portraits as background decoration;
- repeated section numbers as the dominant rhythm;
- component-grid documentation.

## 6. Hard proportion rules

These are production constraints, not suggestions.

### Desktop

- content max width: **1180px**;
- body reading measure: **58–66ch**;
- H1 target: **64–72px**, never larger than 76px;
- major section heading target: **42–56px**, never larger than 60px;
- section kicker: 11–13px;
- section vertical padding: **72–96px**;
- no single image or headline may occupy more than ~55% of the first viewport height;
- hero human image: max rendered height **520px**;
- hero UI/evidence surface: max rendered width **360px**;
- team portrait: max visible width **220px** and max height **280px**;
- before/after UI evidence: each state max width **460px**.

### 390 / 320

- H1: **44–50px**;
- section heading: **32–40px**;
- body: **16–18px**;
- no heading should consume more than ~45% of viewport height;
- Fernando + UI must remain one relationship without either becoming a full-screen object;
- team portraits max width **170px** at 390 and **150px** at 320;
- evidence pairs must be scannable before scrolling through explanatory text.

## 7. Hero

### Job

Create tension, not spectacle.

### Composition

Desktop:
- left: headline + 2–3 line lead;
- right: one controlled Fernando crop;
- one smaller UI evidence surface intersects or aligns with the image;
- provenance under the visual, not inside a competing card.

The hero must read in this order:

1. headline;
2. Fernando;
3. product evidence;
4. lead/provenance.

No two elements may compete at the same dominant scale.

### Fernando

Use a tighter portrait crop than V2. The person is a protagonist, not a wallpaper.

### Product surface

Show only enough UI to establish the goal-first interaction.

Do not show a full pseudo-phone or multiple options if that makes the visual denser than the story.

## 8. Discovery

### Job

Make the relationship graspable in under five seconds.

Use a compact evidence band:

`SALDO POSITIVO ── mesmo com ── USO DO ROTATIVO`

Then one explanatory paragraph and one source note.

Do not use 7rem typography.

Do not create fake transactions, percentages or charts.

## 9. Fernando / consequence

Do not dedicate another giant “Fernando introduction” section.

Instead:
- use one concise consequence block after Discovery;
- include goal + the question “o que já compete com esse objetivo?”;
- if the image reappears, use a detail/crop, not the same hero scene.

## 10. Product thesis

Show the five-step sequence in one compact horizontal/vertical editorial rail.

No cards.

Each step:
- label;
- one sentence max.

The reader should understand the sequence without reading implementation detail.

## 11. Architecture

The current 4 × 4 node grid is rejected.

V3 should show:
- **4 layers max**;
- **2–3 important nodes per layer**;
- clear flow between person/app → agent → evidence → execution;
- secondary stack detail in a caption/source trail.

The visual should explain the system at a glance, while an accessible text summary preserves detail.

## 12. UX evolution

This remains the craft centerpiece but must stop behaving like four documentation screens.

### Structure

First show a compact index of the four decisions:

1. enter through the decision;
2. explain the effort;
3. consent with purpose;
4. remain legible.

Then present each decision as:

- short problem statement;
- one Before surface;
- one After surface;
- one annotation rail;
- one concise “why it matters”.

### Density rules

- remove repeated explanatory text already visible in UI;
- reduce repeated metadata;
- one pair per visual beat;
- minimum **72px** between pairs desktop / **48px** mobile;
- After state should visually carry the improvement without requiring a paragraph to explain it.

## 13. Prototype

Keep the canonical prototype URL.

Desktop:
- use a framed demo area, but do not make the iframe taller than necessary;
- balance copy and product evidence.

Mobile:
- launch externally if embedding compromises usability.

## 14. Team

The current treatment is rejected as too generic/list-like.

V3 uses an editorial credit band:

- portraits smaller;
- names/contributions always visible;
- varied but controlled crop;
- no circular/default-avatar treatment;
- no five giant portraits fighting for page height;
- mentor visually distinguished without implying hierarchy of ownership.

If source images themselves contain circular crops, the layout should acknowledge that material rather than enlarge them into dominant portraits.

## 15. Colophon

Keep:
- Observamos;
- Exploramos;
- Refinamos depois;
- Ainda é hipótese.

Do not hide the most consequential limitations only inside a disclosure.

The collapsed source trail may remain for deep links, but critical limitations must be visible before it.

## 16. Typography

Use one sans family stack plus monospace evidence metadata.

Do not introduce serif merely to signal “editorial”.

Authorship comes from:
- proportion;
- crop;
- evidence;
- rhythm;
- contrast between narrative and artifact zones.

## 17. Color

Base:
- warm white / paper;
- ink/navy;
- muted gray;
- existing Bússola orange.

Orange remains a decision/action/evidence marker.

Do not add another accent color unless a specific information problem requires it.

## 18. Motion

No motion before static approval.

If activated later:
- max 1–2 entrance transitions;
- no float/parallax;
- reduced-motion complete;
- motion cannot fix a weak composition.

## 19. Representative-proof gate

Before full production, V3 must prove:

1. desktop hero;
2. desktop Discovery;
3. one UX evolution pair;
4. team treatment;
5. 390 hero;
6. 390 UX evolution.

A proof fails if:
- headline/image proportions feel bizarre or competing;
- page relies on oversized type;
- mobile is only a vertical stack;
- architecture/evidence looks like documentation;
- team becomes a directory;
- static composition needs motion to feel intentional.

## 20. Publish-readiness

The final candidate cannot reach Kell unless Agentic Factory's executable evaluator receives:

- independent primary/secondary review runs;
- visual evidence manifest with 1440 / 390 / 320 captures;
- content hash integrity;
- no open material issue;
- no unresolved dissent;
- weighted score >= 9.2;
- every applicable dimension >= 9.0;
- readiness = READY.

## 21. Non-goals

V3 is not:
- a redesign of the Bússola product itself;
- a new brand system;
- a fintech marketing landing page;
- a claim that the PoC is production-ready;
- an excuse to invent new financial data.
